As part of my switch to the Designer role, I had to catch up on a course from the previous term. I decided to take 3D Modelling, a course from the Designer curriculum that I had not previously followed.

Because the course was no longer being taught during that term, I had to study the material independently using the available lectures, documentation, and assignment requirements. This gave me the opportunity to learn the basics of 3D modelling and the workflow used to prepare models for use in games.

For the final assignment, I had to create a treehouse based on a given theme. The model had to be modelled in 3D, UV unwrapped, textured, and prepared for use in Unity and Sketchfab. The model had to meet specific technical requirements, including a polycount between 5,000 and 15,000 polygons and a maximum of one material with a resolution of up to 4096 pixels.

For my assignment, I created an abandoned mechanical treehouse. I modelled and UV unwrapped the entire object in Autodesk Maya. I then used Adobe Substance 3D Painter to create and apply the materials and textures, giving the treehouse its worn and abandoned appearance.

Through this project, I gained a better understanding of the complete 3D asset workflow, from modelling and UV unwrapping to texturing and preparing an asset for use in a game engine. It also gave me experience with learning new 3D tools and techniques independently.
