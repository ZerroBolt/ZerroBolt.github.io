For my first Personal Portfolio assignment / item I wanted to learn more about sound design. In the first year of my study I unfortunately couldn't follow the subject Sound Design because back then I had the role of Engineer who didn't need to follow that subject. Now that I am a Designer I thought it would be useful to at least know something about Sound Design.

I basically followed all the lessons and steps of the actual sound design classes and remade the sounds for a trailer. The original trailer is about 2 minutes long, but my personal goal for Personal Portfolio was to remake the sound of a video of max 1 and a half minutes long. So I only remade those sounds. If I had a bit more time I would have done the full video.

I used Ableton to record and edit all the sounds for the trailer. In the last couple of weeks I learned a lot about sound design, what it means to have a decent setup to create realistic sounds and how to use Ableton and its sound effects.
