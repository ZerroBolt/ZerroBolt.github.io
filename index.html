I also completed two internships, where I gained my first experience working in a professional IT environment and learned how to apply my technical skills to real-world projects.

Throughout my studies, I have worked on a variety of projects involving game design, UI/UX, web development, and interactive experiences. I enjoy exploring how technology and design can work together to create experiences that are both functional and engaging.
