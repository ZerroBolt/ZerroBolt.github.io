Tycho Kerkhofs

I am currently a fourth-year Game Design student at CMGT at Saxion University of Applied Sciences in Enschede. I enjoy combining technology, design, and creativity to create interactive experiences.

Outside of my studies, I also like to experiment with photography, which I often share on my Instagram.

Before starting my current degree, I completed an MBO degree in Applicatie- & Mediaontwikkelaar at Summa College in Eindhoven. During this three-year program, I gained experience with technologies such as C#, SQL, HTML, CSS, and PHP. I also completed two internships, where I gained my first experience working in a professional IT environment and learned how to apply my technical skills to real-world projects.

Throughout my studies, I have worked on a variety of projects involving game design, UI/UX, web development, and interactive experiences. I enjoy exploring how technology and design can work together to create experiences that are both functional and engaging.

On this website, you can explore some of my projects, learn more about my experience through my resume, get in touch with me, or find my other social media profiles.

My Journey

  1. 2018

    Started Applicatie- & Mediaontwikkelaar (MBO)

    Aug. 2018 - Jul. 2021

    I started my MBO education in Applicatie- & Mediaontwikkelaar at Summa College in Eindhoven. During these three years, I built my first foundation in software development and learned to work with technologies such as HTML, CSS, PHP and C#. I successfully completed the programme in 2021.

  2. 2019

    Internship at Bonsai Media

    Aug. 2019 - Feb. 2020

    My first internship I ever did was at Bonsai Media in Nederweert (the Nederlands). I mainly worked with WordPress, and I often needed to change the styling in CSS.

  3. 2021

    Internship at Count-IT

    Feb. 2021 - Jun. 2021

    My second internship I did was at Count-IT in Herkenbosch (the Netherlands). This company focusses on creating cash register systems. My task was to make a quick and easy to use debug functionality to the app, so that bugs were a lot easier to fix. I had to work in C# to make everything. I also had to connect the application to actual cash registers and ATM's. I learned a lot about C# and the professional IT industry in this internship.

  4. 2021

    Started Informatica (HBO)

    Sep. 2021 - Oct. 2022

    I started the study Informatica at Avans in 's-Hertogenbosch, where I initially enjoyed working on programming and technical projects. However, during my first and second year, I realised that I was no longer enjoying the way the programme was structured or the projects I was working on.

    I decided to leave the programme and take a gap year, during which I worked at a supermarket. During this time, I discovered that I still wanted to work with technology and programming, but in a more creative way. This eventually led me to CMGT in Enschede.

  5. 2023

    Started CMGT (HBO)

    Sep. 2023

    I started my studies in Creative Media & Game Technologies (CMGT) in Enschede. I initially started as an Engineer, focusing mainly on programming and the technical side of game development.

  6. 2024

    Switch to Designer

    May. 2024

    During my first year, I realised that the increasingly technical side of the programme wasn't the right fit for me. I found myself more interested in the creative aspects of game development, so I decided to switch from the Engineer role to the Designer role.

  7. 2025

    Big Monster Project

    Jun. 2025

    For the final project of my second year, my team and I worked on a Monster Project based on drawings made by children. This was our first large-scale project, lasting an entire 10-week term, compared to the two- to three-week projects we had worked on before.

  8. 2026

    Japan

    Jan. 2026 - Jun. 2026

    As part of my studies, I spent around six months in Japan to learn the language and experience Japanese culture firsthand. This was an opportunity to step outside my usual environment and challenge myself in a completely different setting.

    Read more about my time in Japan in the project here...

    Last Big Project & Internship

    Sep. 2026 - Jan. 2027

    As part of my last year I have to participate in a big project and need to work on something for a real customer.
